Scents and sticks

What's the difference between Bonfire and Yoga Studio?

Bonfire is agarwood: dark resin and smoke, made for after dark. Yoga Studio is palo santo: warm resin with a citrus edge, made for a reset. Not sure? Take the Smell Test or get the Duo.

What are the sticks made of?

Aromatic wood powder, a natural bark binder and water. No synthetic fragrance, no added perfume and no dyes. There's no bamboo core; it's wood all the way through.

How many sticks come in a pack, and how long do they burn?

50 sticks per box (25 g). Each stick is 210 mm (8.25 in) and burns for about 45 minutes.

Where are they made?

In Taiwan. The sticks are extruded and sun-dried, the Japanese way.

Burning and safety

Do I need a holder?

Yes. Burn incense in a stable, heat-resistant holder that catches falling ash. Ours are made for our 210 mm sticks.

How do I light a stick?

Light the tip, let it catch for a few seconds, then blow out the flame so it glows. Full steps are in How to use incense.

Is it safe around pets and kids?

Keep burning incense out of reach, burn in a ventilated room and never leave a lit stick unattended.
