How it started

HOW MR. SNIFF’S GOT ITS NOSE.

It started the way most good ideas do: a few friends, a late night and one very bad stick of incense.

  1. 01

    The bad stick.

    Somebody lit a stick from a gas-station counter. Ten minutes later the room smelled like a candle shop on fire. We opened every window, and one question hung around longer than the smoke: why does nearly all incense smell like perfume?

  2. 02

    The fix.

    Most of it is soaked in fragrance oil. So we went the other way: aromatic wood, a plant-based binder and nothing poured on top. What you smell is what’s burning. We lit a lot of test batches on porches, in garages and in living rooms. Two made the cut.

  3. 03

    The name.

    Every crew has a friend with a nose for it, the one who knows what’s in the room before their coat is off. We put a moustache on him, called him Mr. Sniff, and made him the boss. He’s never once been wrong about a smell.

Two scents. One nose. Come get a whiff.

Why we started

Incense shouldn’t smell like a perfume counter, and it shouldn’t take itself so seriously. Real wood, nothing poured on top and a little personality in every burn.

OUR POINT OF VIEW

Good scents. A little personality. Here's what goes into making Mr. Sniff's different.

Good incense sets the mood.

We make incense for real spaces and real moments. From slow evenings to last-minute resets, every scent has a scene to call its own.

Two scents. On purpose.

We'd rather make two great scents than twenty forgettable ones. Bonfire for after dark, Yoga Studio for the reset. New ones show up only when they've earned a spot.

Nothing precious about it.

Incense doesn't need to be serious to be good. We keep the ingredients honest and the jokes a little crude, so lighting one feels like a good time, not a ceremony.

WHAT GOES INTO IT

Real wood, simple ingredients, and a whole lot of character in every burn.
