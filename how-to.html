HOW TO BURN INCENSE

Follow these simple steps to light, enjoy, and safely extinguish your incense.

Light the incense tip.

Let the tip burn, then blow out the flame. Ensure it glows and releases a steady stream of smoke.

Rest securely in holder.

Extinguish completely after use.

BURN WITH CARE

A few essential safety precautions to keep in mind while your incense burns, ensuring a safe and enjoyable experience.

Supervision

Never leave burning incense unattended. Always keep it within sight.

Ventilation

Always burn incense in a well-ventilated area to allow proper airflow.

Pets and children

Keep burning incense away from children and pets.

Placement

Keep burning incense away from curtains and other flammable materials.

WHEN THE SMOKE CLEARS

A few easy steps to safely clean up after your incense burns.

  1. 01

    LET IT COOL

    Let the incense stick and ash cool completely before touching or removing anything.

  2. 02

    DISPOSE OF ASH

    Once completely cool, dispose of the ash and any remaining incense safely.

  3. 03

    HOLDER CARE

    Remove leftover residue and clean your holder according to its material and care instructions.
