Who we are

This site is run by Edington Studios LLC, 521 Neola Dr., Pittsburgh, PA 15237 ("Mr. Sniff's", "we", "us"). This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit mrsniffs.co or buy from us.

What we collect

  • Information you give us: name, email, shipping and billing address, phone number, and messages you send us.
  • Order information: what you bought, when, and how you paid. Payments are processed by Shopify Payments and its partners; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Device and usage information: IP address, browser type, pages viewed and how you got here, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

How we use it

  • To process and ship your orders and handle returns.
  • To answer your questions and send order updates.
  • To send marketing emails, only if you've opted in. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.
  • To prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We share information only with services that help us run the store, including Shopify (store platform and checkout), Klaviyo (email and reviews), shipping carriers, and advertising partners Meta and TikTok. We don't sell your personal information.

Cookies

We use cookies to keep your cart working, remember your preferences and measure how the site performs. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some features, like the cart, may stop working.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, and to opt out of targeted advertising. To make a request, email hello@mrsniffs.co. We'll verify your request and respond within the time the law requires.

How long we keep it

We keep order records as long as we need them for accounting and legal purposes, and marketing data until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

Children

This site isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll change the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be announced by email or on the site.

Questions about this policy? Email hello@mrsniffs.co or use the contact form.
