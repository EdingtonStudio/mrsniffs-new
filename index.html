Why Mr. Sniff's

NOTHING BUT WOOD.

Most incense is a bamboo stick dipped in perfume. Ours is wood all the way through.

  • All wood

    Aromatic wood powder, a little odorless bark to hold it together, and water. That's the whole ingredient list.

  • Nothing fake

    No synthetic fragrance, no perfume dip, no dyes. The smell is the oil that was already in the wood.

  • Nothing to hide

    Pressed and sun-dried in Taiwan, the Japanese way. Our palo santo comes from trees that fell on their own.
