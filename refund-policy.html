Return window

You can return unopened items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price.

Opened incense

Once a box is opened or a stick is burned, we can't take it back. If a scent just isn't for you, tell us anyway. We read every note and we'll make it right where we can. Damaged or wrong items are always on us.

Holders

Holders can be returned within 30 days if they're unused and in their original packaging. If a holder arrives chipped or broken, send us a photo and we'll replace it at no cost.

How to start a return

  • Email hello@mrsniffs.co with your order number and what you'd like to return.
  • We'll reply with return instructions.
  • Return shipping is on you, unless we sent the wrong or a damaged item.
  • Pack it up and ship it within 14 days of our reply.

Refunds

Once your return arrives and is checked, we refund the original payment method within 5–10 business days. Original shipping costs are not refundable.

Exchanges

The fastest way to swap a scent is to return the unopened box and place a new order.

Questions about this policy? Email hello@mrsniffs.co or use the contact form.
